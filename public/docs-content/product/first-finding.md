# Your first Finding

Start from [Expeditions](/expeditions), open the active expedition, and select a target in its grid or timeline. You can browse and test a local file without a wallet. Publishing a Finding requires a connected wallet on Ethereum mainnet.

## Already own a recognized artifact?

If the expedition already recognizes your Ethscription—for example, the original January 2009 `bitcoin.ico`—open that recovered target and connect the wallet that **currently owns** it. The original ethscribing wallet may no longer be its owner.

Choose **Deposit into marketplace**, review the existing Ethscription and gas in your wallet, and confirm the deposit. This transfers that Ethscription into market custody. You do not upload the file again, create another Ethscription, or publish a new Finding for its existing catalogue assignment.

Wait for Ethereum confirmation and then ownership/index verification. Do not repeat a confirmed deposit while indexing is pending. Once custody is verified, open [Field Wallet](/wallet) to set a price or withdraw the artifact. Depositing does not list it automatically.

The steps below cover submitting a candidate against an unresolved target.

## 1. Follow the clues

Read the target's date, historical path, format, and recovery notes. Preserve the original file and record where you found it. Opening and resaving an XPM, PNG, or ICO can change its bytes even when it looks the same.

Open targets intentionally withhold their reference hash and direct download location. The goal is to follow historical evidence and test a candidate you recovered. The match check confirms the file without handing out the answer.

## 2. Test your candidate

Choose **Submit a Finding**, then select a source:

- **Upload:** choose the original file from your device.
- **Existing Ethscription:** connect a wallet and select a supported Ethscription you own, including a matching item already in market custody.

Testing prepares the target's required file wrapper automatically, hashes the candidate, checks the reference where known, and checks the canonical protocol content plus the disclosed common aliases. Testing sends no Ethereum transaction and costs no gas.

For a lost target, a format check only means the candidate can be submitted for research. It cannot authenticate bytes for which no reference exists.

## 3. Create or deposit

If your candidate passes and no checked duplicate blocks it, the next action prepares a transaction. Review the destination and gas in your wallet.

| Your starting point | Next action |
|---|---|
| Local file without an existing checked match | Create its Ethscription directly into Ethscribe market custody |
| Matching Ethscription in your wallet | Deposit that existing Ethscription into market custody |
| Matching Ethscription already in your market custody | Continue to the signed assignment after custody verification |
| Matching Ethscription owned by someone else | The site does not create another copy or assign their asset to you |

For direct creation, your wallet is the Ethscription creator and the market is its initial owner. The contract preserves a withdrawal path to the entitled owner. Creating or depositing does not list the asset for sale.

## 4. Wait for verification

Ethereum confirmation and the Ethscriptions index are separate steps. The site waits for the applicable five-block safety window and for ownership and contract state to agree.

If the transaction has succeeded but verification is pending, keep its transaction link. Use **Recheck submitted transaction · no gas** to retry verification without creating or depositing again. Resume the existing-Ethscription path if you leave the page after confirmation.

Preflight cannot reserve a payload. If another transaction creates the canonical content first, your transaction can produce a **Finding Receipt** recording the attempt instead. That receipt is not the target artifact, and gas is not refunded. See [creation and intake](artifact-intake.md).

## 5. Sign and publish

Review the claim summary and primary source URL, then sign the Finding assignment. This signature costs no gas and cannot move your Ethscription.

The server verifies the signature, indexed content, target match, and custody before recording the Finding. A successful exact-match Finding updates the target in the expedition. Creating an Ethscription alone does not complete this final assignment.

A lost-file candidate remains a research candidate. It does not automatically turn the unresolved target into an accepted historical recovery.

## After submission

Open [Field Wallet](/wallet) to see the artifact's expedition assignment, withdraw it, or prepare a fixed-price listing. A direct creation needs a one-time **Register for sale** transaction before it can be priced. If sold, the seller's proceeds appear as claimable credit in the wallet.

The expedition's green state records that a matching Ethscription has been recognized. It does not mean the artifact is still escrowed or currently for sale; the listing card checks that separately.
